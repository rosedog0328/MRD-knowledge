AccuScan AccuScan

2024 年的 WGS MRD 方法（EMBO Mol Med）。用 RCA concatemer 讓錯誤校正在單一 read 內完成，錯誤率 4.2e-7、LOD 達 cVAF 1e-6。背景錯誤率由樣本自身量測、門檻由統計模型反推，皆不需 control 世代。
A 2024 WGS MRD method (EMBO Mol Med) using RCA concatemers so that error correction happens within a single read, reaching an error rate of 4.2e-7 and an LOD of cVAF 1e-6. Its background error rate is measured from the sample itself and its threshold derived from the statistical model, neither requiring a control cohort.

相關模組：導覽基本介紹標的與驗證壓噪音分數與門檻評估紀律三條主線我們的位置

另見：RCA、repeat confirmation、nominal specificity

allele 等位基因

同一個 locus 上可能出現的其中一種序列版本。在一般二倍體常染色體位點，個體通常有兩份 allele；性染色體、拷貝數變化與腫瘤基因體可能不符合這個簡化模型。

在 VCF 裡 allele 被編號：0 是 reference allele，1 是第一個 alternate allele，以此類推。因此，在一般二倍體位點，genotype 0/1 表示一份 reference allele 與一份 alternate allele；0|1 則進一步標示相位。
One of the alternative sequence versions at a locus. At a typical diploid autosomal locus, an individual has two allele copies; sex chromosomes, copy-number changes and tumour genomes may differ.

另見：MRD

aneuploidy 非整倍體

染色體數目異常。腫瘤裡非常普遍，也可能使 cellular purity 與 DNA fraction 不一致。
An abnormal chromosome count. It is common in tumours and can make cellular purity differ from tumour DNA fraction.

另見：ploidy

background error rate 背景錯誤率

在確定無腫瘤的樣本上，每個鹼基被誤讀成非參考鹼基的比率。它決定了偵測極限的下界。各方法實測值：未過濾 Illumina WGS 約 1e-3、read-pair 校正 2.8e-5、duplex 7.7e-7、ONT simplex 約 6.7e-3。
The rate at which a base is miscalled as non-reference in samples known to be tumour-free. It bounds the achievable detection limit. Measured values: unfiltered Illumina WGS about 1e-3, read-pair corrected 2.8e-5, duplex 7.7e-7, and ONT simplex about 6.7e-3.

相關模組：基本介紹

另見：position-specific error、limit of blank、Q-score

background polishing 背景拋光

iDES 提出的純計算去噪法，不依賴分子條碼、不依賴 paired-end、不依賴任何 Illumina 專屬特性 —— 因此是本專案最直接可移植的降噪手段。

建模：用 n=12 個高背景的健康 cfDNA 對照（pre-duplication-removal 資料，因為立體型背景無法被分子條碼可靠壓抑）。對 selector 內每一個可能的 SNV，排除 germline SNP 後，蒐集各對照樣本的 AF 成向量 v，移除 v 的最大值以降低 outlier 影響。

分布選擇：若 v 中非零 AF 少於 5 個，用 Gaussian 建模（μ、σ）；否則對非零 AF 擬合 Weibull（R fitdistrplus），另存非零比例以納入 zero-inflation。

拋光：新樣本的候選 allele，Gaussian 走單尾 z 檢定、Weibull 走 pweibull 累積機率。不是無差別剔除易錯鹼基，只在候選變異與 null 分布無法區分時才移除。

⚠️ 需要 n=12 的陰性訓練樣本；本專案目前只有 5 個獨立陰性個體。
A purely computational denoising method from iDES: using a cohort of negative controls, a background distribution is fitted for every position and substitution type (Gaussian if fewer than five non-zero AFs, otherwise a Weibull fit, plus the zero fraction). Candidate variants in a new sample are removed if indistinguishable from that null.

另見：position-specific error、Weibull background model、zero-inflated

BAF B 等位基因分率

別名：B-allele frequency

雜合 germline SNP 位點上，兩個等位基因的相對比例。腫瘤的等位基因失衡會使其偏離 0.5，因此能偵測 copy-neutral LOH —— 這是純看覆蓋度會漏掉的。
The relative proportion of the two alleles at a heterozygous germline SNP. Tumour allelic imbalance shifts it away from 0.5, allowing detection of copy-neutral LOH, which depth-only analysis would miss.

另見：CNA、cnLOH

BAM

別名：SAM、CRAM

已比對到參考基因體的 read 的二進位格式（SAM 的壓縮版）。每一列是一條 read，帶著它的位置、CIGAR、品質與各種 tag。
Binary format for reads aligned to the reference. One record per read, carrying position, CIGAR, quality and tags.

另見：MRD

binomial detection model 二項偵測模型

MRDetect 的核心式：M = N·(1 − (1 − TF)^cov) + μ·R，其中 M 為偵測到的位點數、N 為 compendium 大小、cov 為局部覆蓋、μ 為噪音率、R 為檢查的 read 數。可反推 TF。
MRDetect's core equation: M = N(1 − (1 − TF)^cov) + μR, where M is the number of detected sites, N the compendium size, cov the local coverage, μ the noise rate and R the number of reads examined. It can be inverted to estimate TF.

另見：detection probability model、tumor fraction、detection rate

blacklist 黑名單

從 compendium 中剔除的問題位點清單。判定方式各異：MRDetect 用「≥2 個 control 出現」，INVAR 用「>10% 對照樣本出現或平均背景 >1% MAF」（排除 0.5% 位點）。⚠️ 本專案在 ONT 上用 ≥2 準則會砍掉 5% 位點。
A list of problematic sites removed from the compendium. Criteria vary: MRDetect uses presence in two or more controls; INVAR uses presence in more than 10% of control samples or a mean background above 1% MAF, excluding 0.5% of sites. Note that the same criterion removes 5% of sites on ONT data in this project.

相關模組：標的與驗證壓噪音完整矩陣

另見：compendium、background error rate、position-specific error

breadth supplants depth 廣度取代深度

MRDetect 的核心論證：因為 GE 有物理上限，加深定序在低 TF 時毫無幫助；改為在全基因體上累加數千個位點的微弱訊號，才能突破偵測極限。
The core argument of MRDetect: because genomic equivalents are physically limited, increasing sequencing depth does not help at low TF. Integrating weak signal across thousands of genome-wide sites is what breaks the detection barrier instead.

另見：genomic equivalents、compendium、read-centric

CAPP-Seq CAPP-Seq

Stanford 2014 年的方法（Nat Med），整個 patient-specific panel 家族的共同祖先。用演算法設計 selector 使每位病人都被覆蓋到多個突變。LLOD 約 0.02%。⚠️ stage I 敏感度僅 50% —— 這個限制催生了後續十年的方法演進。
The 2014 Stanford method (Nat Med) and common ancestor of the patient-specific panel family, using an algorithmically designed selector so that most patients are covered at several mutations. LLOD about 0.02%. Its stage I sensitivity of only 50% motivated a decade of subsequent method development.

另見：selector、reporter、iDES

cfDNA 游離 DNA

別名：cell-free DNA、ccfDNA

血漿中游離的短 DNA 片段，主要由凋亡與壞死的細胞釋出，長度以約 166 bp 為眾數（對應核小體）。健康人與癌症病人都有。
Short DNA fragments circulating freely in plasma, released mainly by apoptotic and necrotic cells, with a modal length of about 166 bp reflecting nucleosome protection. Present in both healthy and cancer subjects.

另見：ctDNA、genomic equivalents、fragmentomics

ClairS ONT 體細胞變異呼叫器

專為長讀長資料設計的深度學習 somatic caller，需要腫瘤與配對正常樣本兩份輸入。本專案用它產生 compendium 的候選位點（約 11 萬個）。與文獻常用的 MuTect／Strelka／LoFreq 概念相同、工具不同；因本專案只有單一 caller，無法比照文獻做多 caller 共識過濾，偽陽性須改由後續的 germline 過濾階梯承擔。
A deep-learning somatic variant caller designed for long-read data, taking a tumour sample and a matched normal as input. This project uses it to generate the candidate sites for the compendium (roughly 110,000). It is conceptually equivalent to the MuTect/Strelka/LoFreq callers common in the literature. Because this project has only a single caller, multi-caller consensus filtering is not available and false positives must instead be absorbed by the downstream germline filtering ladder.

相關模組：標的與驗證

另見：compendium、germline filtering ladder

clonal hematopoiesis 克隆性造血

別名：CH

血球細胞中出現的體細胞突變克隆，隨年齡增加而常見。它會出現在 cfDNA 中並被誤判為腫瘤來源，是 tumor-naive 方法必須處理的主要混淆來源。⚠️ 本專案用細胞株，無此問題。
Somatic mutant clones arising in blood cells, increasingly common with age. They appear in cfDNA and can be mistaken for tumour-derived signal, making them the principal confounder for tumour-naive methods. Not an issue in this project, which uses cell lines.

相關模組：壓噪音

另見：tumor-naive、cfDNA

CNA 拷貝數變異

別名：SCNA、CNV、copy number alteration

染色體片段的擴增或缺失。作為 MRD 訊號的優勢是片段大、即使極低覆蓋也看得到；劣勢是靈敏度天花板低 —— ichorCNA 的偵測下限為 TF 3%，NanoRCS 亦報告 TF <3% 時 CNA 失效。
Amplification or deletion of chromosomal segments. As an MRD signal its advantage is large segment size, visible even at very low coverage; its weakness is a low sensitivity ceiling — ichorCNA's detection floor is TF 3%, and NanoRCS likewise reports CNA failing below TF 3%.

相關模組：三條主線我們的位置

另見：ichorCNA、BAF、tumor fraction

cnLOH 拷貝數中性雜合性缺失

別名：copy-neutral LOH

一個等位基因缺失、同時另一個複製補上，總拷貝數不變但雜合性喪失。只看 read depth 完全看不到，必須靠 BAF。
Loss of one allele accompanied by duplication of the other, leaving total copy number unchanged but heterozygosity lost. It is invisible to read-depth analysis alone and requires BAF.

另見：BAF、CNA

CoLoRSdb 長讀長族群變異庫

以長讀長定序建立的族群變異資料庫，本專案用作 panel of normals（PON）。作用是補抓 dbSNP／gnomAD 等短讀長來源系統性漏掉的 ONT germline 變異。本專案的 germline 過濾階梯中，它是臨門一腳：加入後 cross-sample 的雜訊比值由自家 TF0 的 40 倍降至 1.1 倍。
A population variant database built from long-read sequencing, used here as a panel of normals. Its role is to catch ONT germline variants that short-read-derived resources such as dbSNP and gnomAD systematically miss. It is the decisive final step of this project's germline filtering ladder: adding it brought the cross-sample noise ratio down from 40x the in-house TF0 level to 1.1x.

相關模組：標的與驗證

另見：germline filtering ladder、compendium

compendium 變異圖譜

別名：mutation compendium、變異集合

病人（或細胞株）專屬的體細胞變異位點集合，是 tumor-informed 偵測要追蹤的目標。規模從數百（panel）到數萬（WGS）不等。本專案現行版本為 35,603 個位點。
The patient-specific (or cell-line-specific) set of somatic variant sites that tumour-informed detection tracks. Sizes range from hundreds (panel) to tens of thousands (WGS). The current version in this project contains 35,603 sites.

相關模組：基本介紹標的與驗證壓噪音完整矩陣我們的位置

另見：tumor-informed、reporter、germline filtering ladder

concatemer 串聯體

RCA 產生的長分子，內含同一原始模板的多份首尾相連拷貝。ONT 的長讀長在此有結構優勢 —— read 越長，一條 read 內能容納的拷貝越多，共識越可靠（NanoRCS 中位 6 個 subread）。
The long molecule produced by RCA, containing multiple head-to-tail copies of the same original template. ONT's long reads confer a structural advantage here: longer reads hold more copies and yield more reliable consensus (NanoRCS reports a median of six subreads).

相關模組：壓噪音完整矩陣我們的位置

另見：RCA、repeat confirmation、subread

consensus calling 共識呼叫

從同一分子的多份觀測中決定最可能的真實鹼基。NanoRCS 要求 ≥3 個 repeat 一致；AccuScan 要求所有拷貝一致且 ≥2 份支持。
Determining the most likely true base from multiple observations of the same molecule. NanoRCS requires agreement across at least three repeats; AccuScan requires all copies to agree with at least two supporting.

另見：repeat confirmation、concatemer、single-strand consensus

control cohort 對照世代

用於估計噪音水準或決定門檻的陰性樣本集合。MRDetect 用 30 個 control plasma、iDES 用 12 個、ichorCNA 用 27 個健康捐贈者。⚠️ 本專案只有 5 個獨立陰性個體，這是主要約束。
The set of negative samples used to estimate noise levels or set thresholds. MRDetect used 30 control plasmas, iDES 12, and ichorCNA 27 healthy donors. This project has only five independent negative individuals, which is a principal constraint.

相關模組：基本介紹壓噪音分數與門檻我們的位置

另見：cross-sample normal、nominal specificity、cross-patient control

cosine similarity 餘弦相似度

兩個突變特徵向量之間的相似度量。Landau 第三代以此驗證降噪效果：對 UV 相關的 SBS7a，相似度隨降噪層級由 0.21 升至 0.35 再到 duplex 的 0.94。
A similarity measure between two mutational signature vectors. Landau's third generation used it to validate denoising: similarity to the UV-associated SBS7a rose from 0.21 to 0.35 and then to 0.94 across denoising levels up to duplex.

另見：mutational signature、duplex sequencing

coverage 覆蓋度／深度

別名：depth

某個位置被多少條 read 覆蓋。50× 表示平均每個位置約有 50 條 read 覆蓋，不代表它們均支持同一 allele。
How many reads span a position. 50x means on average 50 reads per position.

另見：read、VAF

CpG

序列上一個 C 後接一個 G（p 代表兩者之間的磷酸鍵）。哺乳類多數 5mC 位於 CpG，特定細胞或情況亦可見非 CpG 甲基化。
A cytosine followed by a guanine. Most mammalian 5mC occurs at CpG sites, although non-CpG methylation is also observed in particular cells and contexts.

相關模組：標的與驗證我們的位置

另見：MRD

cross-patient control 跨病人對照

把 A 病人的變異清單套用到 B 病人的樣本上，當作陰性對照 —— 因為任兩位病人的腫瘤變異幾乎不重疊。INVAR 與 MRD-EDGE 皆採此法，是陰性樣本稀缺時的正規解法。
Applying patient A's variant list to patient B's sample as a negative control, since any two patients' tumour variants scarcely overlap. Used by both INVAR and MRD-EDGE, it is an established solution when negatives are scarce.

相關模組：分數與門檻我們的位置

另見：control cohort、cross-sample normal、specificity

cross-sample normal 跨個體正常樣本

來自不同個體的正常樣本，是本專案真正獨立的陰性檢體（目前 4 個 UCSC normal + held-out COLO829BL）。與 INVAR 的 cross-patient control 是同一個概念。
Normal samples from different individuals, the project's genuinely independent negative specimens (currently four UCSC normals plus a held-out COLO829BL). Conceptually the same as INVAR's cross-patient control.

cross-validation leakage 交叉驗證洩漏

在同一批樣本內做交叉驗證卻宣稱為獨立驗證，導致效能被高估。Lung-CLiP 在 Nature 上明文批評此做法。⚠️ 本專案的等價陷阱是對 read 而非樣本做隨機切分。
Performing cross-validation within a single cohort while claiming independent validation, which inflates apparent performance. Lung-CLiP criticised this explicitly in Nature. The equivalent pitfall in this project is splitting randomly by read rather than by sample.

另見：held-out、sample-level evaluation

ctDNA 循環腫瘤 DNA

別名：circulating tumor DNA

cfDNA 中源自腫瘤的那一部分，帶有腫瘤的體細胞變異。在 MRD 情境下它只佔 cfDNA 的極小比例（常低於百萬分之一），這是所有偵測困難的根源。
The tumour-derived fraction of cfDNA, carrying the tumour's somatic alterations. In the MRD setting it constitutes an extremely small share of total cfDNA, often below one part per million — the root of the detection problem.

相關模組：基本介紹壓噪音

另見：cfDNA、tumor fraction、MRD

cVAF 循環變異等位基因分率

別名：circulating variant allele frequency

血漿中某變異的等位基因分率。與一般 VAF 的差別在量級：germline VAF 約 0.5，而 MRD 情境的 cVAF 常在 10⁻⁵–10⁻⁶ 之間，與定序錯誤率同一個數量級。
The variant allele frequency of a mutation in plasma. It differs from ordinary VAF in magnitude: germline VAF is about 0.5, whereas cVAF in MRD settings is typically 1e-5 to 1e-6, the same order as the sequencing error rate.

相關模組：導覽基本介紹壓噪音三條主線

另見：tumor fraction、background error rate、detection rate

detection probability model 偵測機率模型

iDES 提出的簡潔模型。設 n = 定序到的 GE 數、d = 偵測極限、k = reporter 數，則偵測到至少一個 ctDNA 分子的機率為 p = 1 − e^(−ndk)。可解任一參數，是評估「位點數換靈敏度」的直接工具。
A compact model from iDES. With n genomic equivalents sequenced, detection limit d and k reporters, the probability of detecting at least one ctDNA molecule is p = 1 − exp(−ndk). Any parameter can be solved for, making it a direct tool for assessing how site count buys sensitivity.

相關模組：分數與門檻

另見：reporter、genomic equivalents、binomial detection model

detection rate 偵測率

別名：det_rate

本專案的定義（2026-08-09 定案）：帶到突變（ALT）的 read 數 ÷ 在 compendium 位點上檢查過的 read 數。⚠️ 論文的分子是被偵測到的位點數 M，低 TF 下兩者相同，高 TF 或有 germline 汙染時會分岔。
As defined in this project: the number of ALT-bearing reads divided by the number of reads examined at compendium sites. Note that the papers use the number of detected sites as numerator; the two coincide at low TF but diverge at high TF or under germline contamination.

相關模組：分數與門檻完整矩陣

另見：informative reads、cVAF、Z-score

dilution series 稀釋序列

濕實驗版的混合：把高負荷樣本的 DNA 實際稀釋進陰性樣本。比 in silico 更貼近實況（含建庫變異），但成本高、replicate 少。11 篇 Tier A 論文全部做了某種形式的稀釋序列來量測 LLOD。
The wet-lab counterpart of admixture: physically diluting DNA from a high-burden sample into a negative sample. Closer to reality since it includes library-preparation variation, but costly with few replicates. All eleven Tier A papers performed some form of dilution series to measure LLOD.

相關模組：基本介紹標的與驗證

另見：in silico admixture、LLOD

duplex sequencing 雙股定序

同時定序同一 DNA 分子的正反兩股，只有兩股一致的變異才算真。

能校正只發生在單股上的損傷（如 8-oxoguanine 造成的 G>T 轉換），因此錯誤率遠低於單股共識。實測：Landau 第三代達 7.7e-7、NanoSeq 達 2e-7、CODEC 達 4.5e-7。

⚠️ 代價要一併計入：PhasED-seq 量測顯示 duplex 只回收約 19% 的獨立 cfDNA 片段；Landau 第三代作者亦自承「捕獲的獨立分子數少一個數量級」。且該作者指出，在 tumor-informed 情境下，加大輸入量的標準 WGS 預期可達到比 duplex 更低的偵測極限。
Sequencing both strands of the same DNA molecule and accepting a variant only when both agree. This corrects single-strand damage such as oxidative G-to-T, reaching error rates around 1e-7. The cost is severe: only about 19% of unique molecules are recovered.

相關模組：導覽壓噪音三條主線我們的位置

另見：UMI、single-strand consensus、error suppression

EM algorithm 期望最大化演算法

在有隱藏變數時交替執行「求期望」與「最大化」以擬合參數的方法。ichorCNA 用它同時解出 tumor fraction 與 ploidy。
An iterative method alternating expectation and maximisation steps to fit parameters in the presence of latent variables. ichorCNA uses it to jointly solve for tumour fraction and ploidy.

相關模組：分數與門檻

另見：HMM、ichorCNA

error suppression 誤差抑制

降低定序與建庫錯誤的統稱。三條路線：靠濕實驗化學（UMI、duplex、RCA）、靠統計建模（background polishing、outlier suppression）、靠機器學習（SVM、深度學習）。
The general term for reducing sequencing and library errors. Three routes exist: wet-lab chemistry (UMI, duplex, RCA), statistical modelling (background polishing, outlier suppression), and machine learning (SVM, deep learning).

相關模組：基本介紹

另見：duplex sequencing、background polishing、signal-to-noise enrichment

fragment length entropy 片段長度熵

phased variant 同相變異

別名：PV、phased variants

位於同一條 DNA 分子上的兩個以上體細胞突變。

為什麼有效：單鹼基背景約 1e-5，但同一分子上兩個特定位置同時出錯的機率是兩者相乘。PhasED-seq 實測：iDES 單鹼基 3.3e-5、duplex 單鹼基 1.2e-5，而 2x PV 為 8.0e-7、3x PV 為 3.4e-8。

相對 duplex 的優勢：duplex 只回收 19% 的獨立分子，而間距 <20 bp 的 PV 其 unique depth 幾乎等同樣本整體深度 —— 換句話說 PV 用「同分子巧合」而非「更多分子」換準確度。

ONT 的結構優勢：Illumina 因 read/insert 長度限制，只有 48% 的 PV 間距 <80 bp 能被有效捕捉；ONT 的數 kb read 理論上能捕捉任意間距的 PV。

⚠️ 未經驗證的前提：機率相乘假設錯誤彼此獨立。ONT 的 homopolymer 與甲基化 context 造成的系統性錯誤，可能在同一條 read 上相關聯而使此假設失效 —— 必須實測。
Two or more somatic mutations located on the same DNA molecule. Because the probability of two specific positions erring simultaneously is multiplicative, PV background rates are far lower than single-base rates: 8.0e-7 for doublets and 3.4e-8 for triplets, without requiring molecular barcodes.

另見：PhasED-seq、error suppression、read length advantage

PhasED-seq PhasED-seq

Stanford 2021 年的方法（Nat Biotechnol）。改變偵測單元：不看單鹼基而看同一分子上的多個突變（phased variants），背景率降至 8.0e-7（2x）與 3.4e-8（3x），且不需分子條碼、不犧牲分子回收率。其限制正好是 ONT 的優勢。
The 2021 Stanford method (Nat Biotechnol) that changes the unit of detection: instead of single bases it tracks multiple mutations on the same molecule (phased variants), lowering background to 8.0e-7 for doublets and 3.4e-8 for triplets without molecular barcodes and without sacrificing molecule recovery. Its key limitation is precisely where ONT holds an advantage.

ploidy 倍性

細胞內染色體套數。多數正常常染色體為 2（diploid）；腫瘤可呈現 3、4 或其他非整倍體狀態。
The number of chromosome sets in a cell. Most normal autosomal regions are diploid (2); tumours may be triploid, tetraploid or otherwise non-diploid.

PON 正常樣本面板／族群資料庫（依流程而異）

別名：Panel of Normals、PoN

同一縮寫的兩種用法：
· 本教材在 LongPhase-TO、ClairS-TO 情境中特別稱為 population germline database set：1000G、CoLoRSdb、dbSNP、gnomAD 等族群資料庫的聯集，用於標記族群中常見的遺傳變異。

The abbreviation is workflow-dependent and the two uses are not interchangeable. In this tutorial's tumour-only workflows, PON denotes a population germline database set; in GATK Mutect2, a Panel of Normals (PoN) is built from multiple normal samples to flag recurrent technical artifacts. Either can miss a private germline variant or an unseen artifact, so neither replaces a patient's matched normal.

position-specific error 位置專一錯誤

別名：stereotyped error、立體型錯誤

背景錯誤不是隨機的 —— 特定位置的特定取代類型會反覆出錯。iDES 發現 cfDNA 的背景以 G>T 為大宗（源自 hybrid capture 期間的氧化損傷）。既然有規律，就能建模並移除。
Background errors are not random: specific substitutions recur at specific positions. iDES found that cfDNA background is dominated by G-to-T changes arising from oxidative damage during hybrid capture. Because the pattern is systematic, it can be modelled and removed.

prevalence 盛行率

樣本中陽性所佔的比例。本專案的陽性/陰性數量是人為決定的，不代表任何真實盛行率，因此 Precision 與 F1 只在本設計內可比，跨研究比較須看 Recall 與 Specificity。
The proportion of positives among samples. In this project the positive-to-negative ratio is chosen by design and reflects no real prevalence, so precision and F1 are comparable only within this design; cross-study comparison must rely on recall and specificity.

Q-score 品質分數

Phred 品質分數，Q = −10·log₁₀(錯誤率)。Q21 ≈ 6.7e-3、Q31 ≈ 7.2e-4、Q40 ≈ 1e-4、Q70 ≈ 1e-7。NanoRCS 指出：tumor-naive 的 SNV 分析需要 Q70 以上才能偵測 TF >1%。
The Phred quality score, Q = −10·log10(error rate). Q21 is about 6.7e-3, Q31 about 7.2e-4, Q40 1e-4 and Q70 1e-7. NanoRCS notes that tumour-naive SNV analysis requires Q70 or better to detect TF above 1%.

R1/R2 concordance 雙端一致性

Illumina paired-end 定序中，同一片段的兩端 read 在重疊區是否給出相同鹼基。不一致即視為錯誤。ONT 無 read pair，此特徵與其對應的過濾完全不可移植 —— 這是 MRDetect 去噪在 ONT 上受阻的主因之一。
In Illumina paired-end sequencing, whether the two reads of a fragment agree at overlapping bases; disagreement indicates error. ONT has no read pairs, so this feature and its associated filter are entirely non-transferable — a principal reason MRDetect's denoising is blocked on ONT.

RCA 滾環擴增

別名：rolling circle amplification

把 DNA 環化後沿環複製，產生含同一模板多份串聯拷貝的長分子（concatemer）。因此共識可以在單一 read 之內完成，不需要多條 read 或正反股配對。AccuScan 與 NanoRCS 皆採此法。
Circularising DNA and replicating around the circle to produce a long molecule containing multiple tandem copies of the same template. Consensus can therefore be formed within a single read, without needing multiple reads or paired strands. Used by both AccuScan and NanoRCS.

read

定序儀產生的一段序列觀測。一條 read 通常來自一個 DNA 分子的片段，但不等同於一個細胞；仍需考慮重複、嵌合與定序錯誤。
A sequence observation produced by the sequencer, usually a fragment from one DNA molecule. A read is molecular evidence, not a cell; duplicates, chimeras and sequencing errors still need to be considered.

read family 讀取家族

The set of reads sharing a UMI and therefore originating from the same molecule. INVAR requires a minimum family size of two; raising the threshold lowers error further at the cost of discarding more data.

read length advantage 讀長優勢

ONT 的數 kb read 相對 Illumina 的 150 bp，在需要「同一分子上多個位點」的方法（如 phased variants）上具結構性優勢：能捕捉的位點組合多出數個數量級，且不損失任何分子。
ONT's multi-kilobase reads confer a structural advantage over Illumina's 150 bp reads for methods requiring multiple sites on the same molecule, such as phased variants: orders of magnitude more site combinations are captured, without discarding any molecules.

read-centric 以 read 為單位

與 locus-centric 相對：一次判斷的對象是一條 read，問「這一條 read 帶的突變是真的還是讀錯的」，證據來自這條 read 自身的性質（鹼基品質、比對品質、突變在 read 上的位置）。之所以必須這樣改，是因為 TF 遠小於 1／深度時每個位點最多只期待一條支持 read，使支持數門檻歸零、且位點層的 VAF 對每個被偵測到的位點都相同而失去鑑別力。
The counterpart to locus-centric: the unit of judgement is a single read, and the question asked is whether the mutation carried by that read is genuine or a sequencing error. The evidence comes from properties of the read itself — base quality, mapping quality, the position of the mutation within the read. The shift is forced because when TF is far below 1/depth at most one supporting read is expected per site, which nullifies supporting-count thresholds and leaves the locus-level VAF identical across every detected site, hence uninformative.

repeat confirmation 重複確認

AccuScan 的判定規則：某個 base call 必須被 read pair 內所有串聯拷貝一致支持、且至少 2 份支持，才算 repeat confirmed；拷貝之間不一致者視為隨機錯誤丟棄。
AccuScan's decision rule: a base call must be consistently supported by all tandem copies within a read pair, with at least two supporting, to count as repeat confirmed. Inconsistencies between copies are discarded as random errors.

reporter 報告位點

Each tracked variant site within the compendium. The core insight of CAPP-Seq is that the more reporters a patient has, the higher the detection probability.

另見：compendium、detection probability model、marker

robust Z-score 穩健標準分數

Replacing mean and standard deviation with median and median absolute deviation: (x − median)/MAD. Insensitive to outliers, this is used by MRDetect's CNA module on 500 bp bins.

A hard rule in this project: the unit for TP/FP/FN/TN is one specimen (one BAM), not one read and not one site. The read-level denoiser's F1 or AUC is an internal quality metric and must not be reported as detection performance.

single-molecule rule 單分子準則

AccuScan's equivalent design: at low tumour burden, genuine somatic variants almost always appear on a single molecule, so sites supported by more than one molecule are excluded. This is the same insight as INVAR's outlier suppression, reached independently.

TF 腫瘤分數的縮寫

Abbreviation for tumour fraction, used throughout this knowledge base. It is the proportion of DNA in a sample that originates from the tumour, and represents signal strength. tumor-naive 無腫瘤資訊

UMI 唯一分子標識

A random sequence tag ligated to each original molecule during library preparation. Reads sharing a tag derive from the same molecule and can be collapsed into a consensus, removing PCR and sequencing errors. Standard ONT workflows do not carry such tags.

VAF 變異等位基因頻率

別名：allele fraction

在某個位點上，支持 alt allele 的 read 佔全部 read 的比例。

最常見的誤解是把 VAF 當成「有多少比例的細胞帶這個突變」。實際上還受到 tumor purity、該位置的 local copy number、mutation multiplicity 與 mapping bias 影響。理想的 diploid、單一拷貝突變情況下：
VAF=purity×multiplicity/total copy number
所以 purity 0.4、diploid、單拷貝時 VAF 只有 0.2。
Variant Allele Frequency: the fraction of reads supporting the alternate allele. Not the same as the fraction of cells carrying it.

Weibull background model Weibull 背景模型

iDES 用於擬合位置專一背景錯誤的分布族。選它的理由是對「非零背景錯誤」的擬合表現穩健；非零觀測少於 5 個時改用 Gaussian。
The distribution family iDES fits to position-specific background errors, chosen for robust performance on non-zero background. When fewer than five non-zero observations are available, a Gaussian is used instead.

Z-score 標準分數

把偵測分數對陰性對照世代的分布標準化：Z = (det_rate − μ)/σ。⚠️ 本專案已降級為選項之一（2026-08-08）—— 常態假設未經檢定，且會把稀缺的陰性樣本綁去估參數。
Standardising a detection score against the distribution of a negative control cohort: Z = (det_rate − μ)/σ. Downgraded to one option among several in this project, since the normality assumption is untested and it consumes scarce negative samples for parameter estimation.

zero-inflated 零膨脹

資料中零值的比例遠高於一般分布所預期。背景錯誤資料典型如此（多數對照樣本在多數位點觀察不到任何錯誤），因此 iDES 額外記錄非零比例來建模。
Data in which zeros occur far more often than a standard distribution would predict. Background error data are typically zero-inflated, since most controls show no error at most positions; iDES therefore records the non-zero fraction separately.
